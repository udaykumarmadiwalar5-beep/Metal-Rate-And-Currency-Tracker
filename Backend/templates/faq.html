Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about MetalTracker, live prices, and our Investment Advisor.

What is MetalTracker and what can I do here?
MetalTracker is a dashboard for tracking Gold, Silver, and Platinum market movements. You can view current prices, historical charts, set personal price alerts, and use the Investment Advisor to explore NSE/BSE company recommendations aligned with your investment goals.
How do I register and log in?
Open the Register page and enter a valid email, username, and password. After registering, use those credentials on the Login page to access your dashboard and saved alerts.
Do I need an account to use the full system?
No, you can browse the public dashboard and FAQ without logging in. But to create alerts, save your preferences, or use the Investment Advisor tools, you must register and log in.
How accurate are the metal prices on the dashboard?
Prices are based on the latest stored data in the system. The dashboard shows refreshed market rates and provides charts for recent trends, but actual market conditions may still vary. Use this tool for comparison and tracking, not as a trading execution feed.
How do the historical charts work?
The chart section displays historical price data for the selected metal and timeframe. You can switch between single-metal views and the combined All Metals view to compare performance over different periods.
What is the Investment Advisor feature?
The Investment Advisor helps you discover relevant NSE/BSE companies and SIP plans based on your budget, risk profile, and investment interest. It’s designed to guide you through stock ideas tied to the metals market and broader opportunities.
How do alerts work?
Once logged in, set a target price for Gold, Silver, or Platinum in the Alerts section. The system stores your alert and tracks it automatically. When the current price reaches the condition you selected, the alert status changes from "Watching" to "Triggered".
How can I tell if my alert has been triggered?
Visit the Alerts dashboard after logging in. Each alert shows a clear status label and the current metal price. Triggered alerts are marked as "Triggered," while open targets remain labeled "Watching."
Will I receive email notifications when an alert triggers?
Not yet. The current system updates alert status within the dashboard only. Email notification support is planned for a future release to enhance professional alert delivery.
Can I set alerts for multiple metals?
Yes. You can create separate alerts for Gold, Silver, and Platinum. Each alert is tracked independently and shown in your personal alert list.
How do I manage my dashboard preferences?
After logging in, use the Preferences page to adjust your theme, investment goals, budget, and preferred risk settings. These preferences help personalize recommendations and dashboard display.
Is my account data safe?
Yes. Your registration details are used only to authenticate you and save your dashboard settings. The system does not share your password or personal alert targets with third parties.
