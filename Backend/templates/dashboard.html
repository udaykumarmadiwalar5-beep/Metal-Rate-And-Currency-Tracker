❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about MetalTracker.

How often are metal prices updated? ▼
Metal prices are refreshed every 60 seconds during market hours, sourcing data from MCX and COMEX global commodity exchanges.
What does the AI Recommendation feature do? ▼
The AI engine analyses historical price trends, news sentiment, and market volatility to generate buy/hold/watch signals. These are indicative and not financial advice.
How do I set price alerts? ▼
Go to Settings → Alerts. Set target prices for any metal and get notified when the price crosses your threshold.
Which currencies are supported? ▼
Currently INR, USD, EUR, and GBP. More currencies including AED, JPY, and SGD are coming soon.
Can I export price charts? ▼
Yes — right-click any chart to save as PNG. CSV export of historical data is coming in the next update.
Is MetalTracker free? ▼
Basic tracking is free. AI Recommendations and advanced analytics need a Pro subscription at ₹299/month.
